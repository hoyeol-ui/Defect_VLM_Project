# Human Participation Checklist

Protocol version: `gc10-cost-translation-v1-candidate`

This checklist is not legal or ethics approval. Obtain a formal determination from the relevant institution before collecting annotation events or work-time data.

## Institutional determination

- [ ] Identify the responsible IRB/research-ethics office.
- [ ] Submit the protocol summary, participant role, recorded fields, recruitment, compensation, and intended publication.
- [ ] Obtain one written determination: full review, expedited review, exemption, or not-human-subjects research.
- [ ] Record determination ID, date, restrictions, and expiration if applicable.
- [ ] Confirm whether student/employee participation introduces power imbalance or employment-law considerations.

## Consent and information

- [ ] Provide the approved information sheet before participation.
- [ ] Explain that the study records image-level working time and editing actions.
- [ ] Explain that speed is a research variable, not an employment-performance rating.
- [ ] Explain foreseeable fatigue, eye strain, repetitive work, and breaks.
- [ ] Describe voluntary participation and withdrawal.
- [ ] Explain whether already-collected deidentified data can be withdrawn and until what date.
- [ ] Obtain consent in the institutionally approved form.

## Identity and privacy

- [ ] Assign pseudonymous IDs such as `ANN-01`; do not store names/emails in event logs.
- [ ] Store the ID key separately with restricted access.
- [ ] Disable screen recording by default.
- [ ] If screen recording is required, obtain separate explicit approval and consent.
- [ ] Confirm whether keyboard/mouse telemetry is considered sensitive monitoring.
- [ ] Ensure public data contain no direct identifier, workstation account, or email.

## Work conditions

- [ ] Define annotator expertise and required training.
- [ ] Freeze display, browser, input device, zoom controls, guide version, and break schedule.
- [ ] Prevent supervisors from pressuring participation.
- [ ] Define maximum session length and mandatory rest.
- [ ] Provide a contact for questions or adverse effects.

## Compensation

- [ ] Specify compensation amount, unit, payment timing, and treatment of withdrawal.
- [ ] Pay for training, Stage 0, annotation, breaks required by protocol, and adjudication as applicable.
- [ ] Confirm whether employees participate during paid work hours.
- [ ] Do not tie compensation to annotation speed or arm-favorable outcomes.

## Data governance

- [ ] List raw event logs, annotations, correction logs, consent records, and ID key.
- [ ] Define storage location, encryption, access roles, backup, and retention period.
- [ ] Define destruction method and date.
- [ ] Separate research artifacts from the participant identity key.
- [ ] Define what can be placed in the thesis, repository, or public benchmark.
- [ ] Confirm whether raw timestamps can reveal work schedules and therefore require aggregation.

## Release boundary

- [ ] Public release contains only approved deidentified aggregates or artifacts.
- [ ] No consent form, identity key, raw workstation identifier, or unapproved screen recording is committed.
- [ ] Annotator-level tables use pseudonyms and suppress identifying free text.
- [ ] Any dataset redistribution complies with GC10 licensing and institutional policy.
